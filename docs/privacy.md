# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Chessmirror (棋镜) · last updated 2026-10-08

## English

Chessmirror does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting
SDK, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer runs no server.

- **Photos and camera.** Board photos are read on your device. A picture you keep is saved
  beside its game and nowhere else.
- **Games and practice history.** They are files in the app's folder in your own iCloud Drive
  (or on the device when iCloud is off). Apple syncs them between your devices; the developer
  cannot read them.
- **Network.** The app goes online only when you import games: it sends the username or link
  you typed to the site you chose (lichess.org, chess.com, or the site the link points to) and
  downloads the games. Once you have fetched your own account's games, it checks that site for
  new ones once a day. Those requests go from your device straight to that site and are subject
  to that site's privacy policy.
- **Analysis.** The chess engine (Stockfish) runs on your device.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/sunfmin/chessmirror/issues.

## 中文

棋镜不收集任何数据。没有账号，没有统计，没有崩溃上报的 SDK，没有广告，没有追踪，开发者也没有服务器。

- **照片和相机。** 棋盘照片在你的设备上识别。你留下的照片存在它那盘棋旁边，不去别处。
- **对局和练习记录。** 是你自己 iCloud Drive 里这个 App 的文件夹中的文件（没开 iCloud 时在设备上）。
  Apple 在你的设备之间同步它们；开发者读不到。
- **网络。** 只有导入对局时才联网：把你输入的用户名或链接发给你选的棋站（lichess.org、chess.com，
  或链接指向的站），把对局下载回来。拉过一次自己账号的对局之后，每天向那个站查一次有没有新棋。
  这些请求从你的设备直接发到那个站，适用那个站的隐私政策。
- **分析。** 国际象棋引擎（Stockfish）在你的设备上运行。

有问题：到 https://github.com/sunfmin/chessmirror/issues 提一个 issue。
